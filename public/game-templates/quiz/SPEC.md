# The quiz

Rules, as shipped:

- The questions in config/questions.js are asked in order, one screen each.
- Every answer counts one point toward one ending. The ending with the most
  points wins; a tie goes to the ending listed first in RESULTS.
- The player can play again from the ending screen.

Ways to remix without code: change the questions, answers and endings in the
form; change the words in config/words.js; restyle css/style.css; replace the
two sounds. Ways that need a change to js/quiz.js: a timer per question,
answers worth different points, showing a picture per ending, shuffling the
question order.
