The standard set: where each picture came from, and under what.

Every entry in index.json carries its own `by` and `licence`, which is what
the studio shows on the shelf and in the banner when somebody picks one. This
file is the longer version, kept beside the art the way studio/fonts-license.txt
is kept beside the typefaces.

Everything here is CC0 — public domain, no attribution required, commercial
use fine. Credit is given anyway, because somebody drew it.
